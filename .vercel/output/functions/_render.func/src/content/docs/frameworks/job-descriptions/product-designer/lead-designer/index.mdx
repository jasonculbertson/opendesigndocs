## Role Overview
As a Lead Designer at [Company Name], you will be responsible for leading our design team and driving our overall design strategy. You'll manage a team of designers, collaborate with senior leadership, and ensure our product experiences meet the highest standards of quality and usability.

- Lead and manage a team of product designers
- Set design vision and strategy aligned with company goals

- 7+ years of experience in UX/UI design
- 2+ years of people management experience

- Experience with design operations and process improvement
- Strong leadership and communication skills
- Bachelor's degree in Design, HCI, or related field (or equivalent experience)
