## Role Overview
As a Writer II at [Company Name], you will be responsible for creating clear, concise, and engaging content that enhances the user experience across our products and platforms. This mid-level position is ideal for someone who has demonstrated success in content design, has a strong grasp of UX principles, and can work independently while collaborating effectively with cross-functional teams.

## What You'll Do
- Write and maintain clear, concise, and user-friendly content for product interfaces
- Lead content strategy for small to medium-sized projects
- Collaborate with designers, product managers, and engineers to ensure content meets user needs
- Help maintain and evolve content style guides and documentation
- Conduct content audits and make recommendations for improvements
- Mentor junior writers and provide constructive feedback
- Participate in user research to inform content decisions
- Support localization efforts by writing content that is easy to translate
- Document content decisions and rationale

## What You'll Need
- Bachelor's degree in English, Communications, Technical Writing, or related field
- 2-4 years of professional UX writing or content design experience
- Strong portfolio demonstrating excellent writing and content design skills
- Proven ability to write in a clear, concise style for diverse audiences
- Experience with content strategy and information architecture
- Strong understanding of UX principles and content design best practices
- Excellent communication and collaboration skills
- Experience with content management systems
- Ability to work independently and manage multiple projects
- Portfolio of writing samples showing range and impact

## Nice to Have
- Experience with design tools (Figma, Sketch)
- Knowledge of HTML and Markdown
- Experience with technical writing or API documentation
- Understanding of accessibility guidelines
- Experience with A/B testing and content experiments
- Knowledge of SEO best practices
- Experience working in an agile environment
