## Role Overview
As a Writer I at [Company Name], you will be responsible for creating clear, concise, and engaging content that enhances the user experience across our products and platforms. This entry-level position is ideal for someone who is passionate about writing, has a strong grasp of UX principles, and is eager to learn and grow in a collaborative environment.

## What You'll Do
- Write clear, concise, and user-friendly content for product interfaces
- Collaborate with designers and product managers to ensure content meets user needs
- Help maintain content style guides and documentation
- Participate in user research to understand content needs
- Assist in content audits and improvements
- Learn and apply UX writing best practices
- Support localization efforts by writing content that is easy to translate

## What You'll Need
- Bachelor's degree in English, Communications, Technical Writing, or related field
- 0-2 years of professional writing experience
- Strong writing and editing skills with attention to detail
- Basic understanding of UX principles and content design
- Ability to write in a clear, concise style
- Good communication and collaboration skills
- Eagerness to learn and grow professionally
- Portfolio of writing samples

## Nice to Have
- Experience with content management systems
- Knowledge of HTML and Markdown
- Familiarity with design tools (Figma, Sketch)
- Understanding of accessibility guidelines
- Experience with technical writing or documentation
- Knowledge of SEO best practices
