# Open Design Docs

**Free, open-source design career resources for product designers, UX designers, and design managers.**

🌐 Website: [opendesigndocs.com](https://opendesigndocs.com)

---

## What is Open Design Docs?

Open Design Docs is a comprehensive collection of free resources to help design professionals advance their careers and design managers build effective teams. All content is open-source and freely available.

### Who is this for?

- **Product Designers** looking to understand career progression
- **UX Designers** preparing for interviews or performance reviews
- **Design Managers** building teams and evaluating talent
- **Recruiters** hiring for design roles
- **HR Teams** creating design career ladders

### 1. Design Career Ladder (Levels & Titles)

A complete framework for design career progression from entry-level to principal:

| Level | Title | Typical Experience |
|-------|-------|-------------------|
| L1 | Designer 1 / Junior Designer | 0-2 years |
| L2 | Designer 2 | 2-4 years |
| L3 | Senior Designer | 4-6 years |
| L4 | Staff Designer | 6-8 years |
| L5 | Lead Designer | 8-10 years |
| L6 | Principal Designer | 10+ years |

## 📖 Key Concepts Explained

### Individual Contributor (IC) vs. Manager Track
- **IC Track**: Designers who advance through craft expertise (Designer → Senior → Staff → Lead → Principal)
- **Manager Track**: Designers who transition to people management

### Design Ladder
A career progression framework that defines expectations at each level. Unlike ad-hoc promotions, a design ladder provides clear criteria for advancement.

### Competency Matrix
A detailed breakdown of skills and behaviors expected at each career level, typically covering:
- Craft/technical skills
- Collaboration
- Communication
- Leadership/influence
- Strategic thinking

### Interview Panels
Structured interviews with multiple evaluators, each assessing different competencies. Reduces bias and provides comprehensive candidate evaluation.
