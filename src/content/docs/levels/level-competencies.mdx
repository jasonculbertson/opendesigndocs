## How to Use This Framework

The level competencies framework is designed to:

- **Set Clear Expectations**: Each role has specific competencies outlined by level
- **Guide Career Growth**: Use these as roadmaps for skill development and promotions
- **Support Performance Reviews**: Reference these competencies during evaluations
- **Inform Hiring**: Use as guidelines when creating job descriptions and interview panels

Each competency framework includes:
- Core skills and technical abilities
- Leadership and collaboration expectations
- Impact and scope of work
- Communication and presentation skills
- Strategic thinking and business acumen

## Getting Started

1. **Choose Your Role**: Click on the competency framework that matches your current or target role
2. **Assess Current Level**: Review the competencies for your current level
3. **Plan Growth**: Identify skills needed for the next level
4. **Set Goals**: Work with your manager to create development plans
5. **Track Progress**: Use these frameworks during regular check-ins and reviews
