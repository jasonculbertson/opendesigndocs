# Senior Director of Design Operations

## About the Role

As the Senior Director of Design Operations, you will be responsible for leading and scaling design operations across multiple product lines and regions. You will develop and execute strategic initiatives that enable our global design organization to work efficiently and effectively at scale.

## Key Responsibilities

- Drive global design operations strategy
- Lead and scale multiple DesignOps teams
- Develop and optimize enterprise-wide design processes
- Manage complex design tools and systems
- Drive operational excellence across regions
- Partner with executive leadership
- Manage substantial program budgets

## Required Qualifications

- 12+ years of experience in design operations or related field
- Experience leading operations at scale
- Track record of building global teams
- Enterprise design tools expertise
- Large budget management experience
- Executive-level communication skills
- Strategic planning expertise

## Preferred Qualifications

- Experience in Fortune 500 companies
- Advanced degree (MBA preferred)
- Design systems expertise
- International team management
- Change management certification
- Public speaking experience
