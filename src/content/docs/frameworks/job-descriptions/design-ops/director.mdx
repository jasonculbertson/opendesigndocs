# Director of Design Operations

## About the Role

As the Director of Design Operations, you will be responsible for leading and scaling design operations across the organization. You will build and manage the systems, processes, and tools that enable our design team to work efficiently and effectively.

## Key Responsibilities

- Lead and scale design operations strategy
- Build and manage DesignOps team
- Develop and optimize design processes
- Manage design tools and resources
- Drive operational excellence
- Partner with design leadership
- Manage design program budget

## Required Qualifications

- 10+ years of experience in design operations or related field
- Experience building and leading operations teams
- Strong track record of scaling design organizations
- Experience with design tools and systems
- Budget management experience
- Excellent project management skills
- Strong communication abilities

## Preferred Qualifications

- Experience in enterprise software companies
- Knowledge of design systems
- Change management experience
- Agile/lean methodology expertise
- Vendor management experience
- MBA or relevant advanced degree
