## Role Overview
As a Senior Designer at [Company Name], you will be a key leader in shaping our product design strategy and execution. You'll drive major design initiatives, mentor junior designers, and work closely with product leadership to ensure exceptional user experiences.

## What You'll Do
- Lead design strategy for major product initiatives
- Define and document design patterns and best practices
- Mentor and provide technical guidance to junior designers
- Drive improvements to our design process
- Collaborate with product and engineering leadership
- Lead user research initiatives
- Advocate for design best practices
- Contribute to hiring and team growth

## What You'll Need
- 5+ years of experience in UX/UI design
- Proven track record of leading successful design projects
- Strong portfolio demonstrating strategic design thinking
- Excellence in interaction design and visual design
- Deep understanding of design systems
- Experience mentoring junior designers
- Strong project management skills
- Bachelor's degree in Design, HCI, or related field (or equivalent experience)
