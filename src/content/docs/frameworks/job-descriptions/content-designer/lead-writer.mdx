# Lead Content Designer

## About the Role

As a Lead Content Designer, you will be responsible for leading content strategy and execution across our product suite. You will work closely with product, design, and engineering teams to create clear, consistent, and compelling content that enhances the user experience.

## Key Responsibilities

- Lead content strategy and execution for major product initiatives
- Mentor and guide junior content designers
- Establish and maintain content guidelines and standards
- Collaborate with cross-functional teams to ensure content quality
- Drive content research and testing initiatives
- Contribute to product strategy and decision-making

## Required Qualifications

- 7+ years of content design experience
- Strong portfolio demonstrating content strategy and execution
- Experience leading and mentoring content designers
- Excellent written and verbal communication skills
- Strong understanding of UX principles
- Experience with content management systems
- Bachelor's degree in relevant field or equivalent experience

## Preferred Qualifications

- Experience in SaaS or developer tools
- Knowledge of technical writing and API documentation
- Experience with design systems
- Understanding of accessibility standards
- Experience with user research and testing
