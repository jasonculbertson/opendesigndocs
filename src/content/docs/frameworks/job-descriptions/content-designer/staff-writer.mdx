# Staff Content Designer

## About the Role

As a Staff Content Designer, you will be a technical leader in content design, driving complex initiatives and mentoring other designers. You will work on challenging content problems and help shape the future of our product's content strategy.

## Key Responsibilities

- Lead complex content design initiatives
- Drive content strategy for major product areas
- Mentor and guide other content designers
- Collaborate with product and engineering leadership
- Define and document content patterns
- Contribute to design system development

## Required Qualifications

- 8+ years of content design experience
- Strong portfolio demonstrating complex content solutions
- Experience leading content initiatives
- Excellent written and verbal communication skills
- Deep understanding of UX principles
- Experience with content systems and tooling

## Preferred Qualifications

- Experience in enterprise software or developer tools
- Technical writing expertise
- Content system architecture experience
- User research experience
- Public speaking experience

## Skills & Competencies

### Technical Leadership

- Shape product direction
- Establish best practices
