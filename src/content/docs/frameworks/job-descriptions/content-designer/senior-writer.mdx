# Senior Content Designer

## About the Role

As a Senior Content Designer, you will create and maintain high-quality content across our product suite. You will work with product teams to ensure clear, consistent, and effective communication throughout the user experience.

## Key Responsibilities

- Create and maintain product content across multiple features
- Collaborate with product, design, and engineering teams
- Contribute to content strategy and guidelines
- Mentor junior content designers
- Conduct content research and testing
- Document content decisions and processes

## Required Qualifications

- 5+ years of content design experience
- Strong portfolio demonstrating content strategy and execution
- Experience with UX writing and content strategy
- Excellent written and verbal communication skills
- Understanding of UX principles
- Experience with content management systems

## Preferred Qualifications

- Experience in SaaS or developer tools
- Knowledge of technical writing
- Experience with design systems
- Understanding of accessibility standards
- Experience with user research

## Skills & Competencies

### Technical
- Content strategy
- UX writing
- Information architecture
- Style guides
- Content management systems
- User research methods

### Soft Skills
- Problem-solving
- Communication
- Collaboration
- Time management
- Mentorship

## Impact & Scope

- Drive content quality for product features
- Contribute to content strategy
- Mentor junior team members
- Improve content processes
- Participate in user research

### Potential Next Steps
