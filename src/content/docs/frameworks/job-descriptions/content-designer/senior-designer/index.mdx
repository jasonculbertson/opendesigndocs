## Role Overview
As a Senior Designer at [Company Name], you will be a key leader in shaping our product content strategy and voice. You'll drive major content initiatives, mentor junior writers, and work closely with product and design leadership to ensure exceptional content experiences across our platform.

## What You'll Do
- Lead content strategy for major product initiatives and features
- Define and document content standards, patterns, and best practices
- Mentor and provide technical guidance to junior writers
- Drive improvements to our content creation and management processes
- Collaborate with design and product leadership on strategic decisions
- Lead content research initiatives and user testing
- Advocate for content best practices across the organization
- Contribute to hiring and team growth initiatives

## What You'll Need
- 5+ years of experience in UX writing, content design, or related field
- Proven track record of leading successful content initiatives
- Strong portfolio demonstrating strategic content thinking
- Excellence in writing, editing, and content strategy
- Deep understanding of content design systems and information architecture
- Experience mentoring junior writers
- Strong project management and communication skills
- Bachelor's degree in English, Communications, or related field (or equivalent experience)
