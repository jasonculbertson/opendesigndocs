## Role Overview
As a Designer I at [Company Name], you will create visually compelling designs for various digital and print mediums. This entry-level position is ideal for someone who is passionate about visual design, has a strong creative foundation, and is eager to learn and grow in a collaborative environment.

- Create visual designs for digital and print materials

- Assist in the development of marketing materials

- Participate in design reviews and feedback sessions

- 0-2 years of experience in graphic design

- Attention to detail and organizational skills
- Bachelor's degree in Graphic Design or related field (or equivalent experience)
